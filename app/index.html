      How this guide is built

      Every guide is written in our own words from the launch-day guides listed above, in numbered steps, each step citing its source. Where two sources disagree on a number or a condition, both versions are shown and neither is picked. Where no source gives a method, the trophy is marked unconfirmed instead of guessed. Official trophy names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them.
