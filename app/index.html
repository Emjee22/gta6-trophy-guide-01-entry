    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — there are 15 manual slots.

    Step 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

    Play the five chapters with Jason and Lucia and take side content as it appears. Optional missions, vehicle scans (200 of 277 for 100%) and animal studies (100 of 255) already count toward the checklist, even though you cannot see it yet. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia before the chapter ends — it is the one thing that can be missed.

    • Story trophies: 12 (chapters 1–5 and the named missions)
    • Character quests start here: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Cal
    • Scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app; study animals with binoculars

    Step 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the story, open Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: most side quests and many collectibles are not required, or only partly. Everything on it can still be done in free roam.

    Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and clean-up 10–20 hours

    Stranger strands are not part of 100%, so finish the 8 that count, then the character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) and whatever is left on the checklist. The platinum pops with the last trophy you earn.

    • Hardest trophy: A Long Road (100% completion)
    • Last clean-ups: challenges, stunts, card sets

    Time estimates: PowerPyx puts the platinum at about 200 hours; Push Square says 250 hours or more. Both agree on difficulty 4/10 and one playthrough.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy can be missed. Everything else can be finished after the story in free roam.

    Positive Mental Attitude MISSABLE

    Meditate at every spot across Leonida. There are 18 meditation spots. The first one is missable: in Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen at Jason's house. She says she is going to meditate and walks to the balcony — follow her, talk to her there and choose Accept. After Chapter 1 it is gone for good, and there is no mission replay.

    It is still unconfirmed whether that first meditation is actually required for the trophy: it raises the progress counter by 1 but is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the rest. PowerPyx, GTABase and KeenGamer all say the same thing — do it anyway, and make a manual save before you move on. For the 100% checklist you only need 15 of 18 meditations, so the doubt is only about this trophy.

    Everything else: optional missions disappear over time and some quest givers go quiet for a while, but every trophy-related character comes back later, and no other trophy depends on a moment you can lose.

    Where the sources disagree

    • Hidden count: 28 hidden trophies (PowerPyx, GTABase, Insider Gaming, HowToVideoGame) against 27 (Push Square). This guide follows the 28 count: 12 story trophies plus 16 side ones.
    • Time to platinum: about 200 hours (PowerPyx, KeenGamer, GTABase) against 250 hours or more (Push Square).
    • The final mission's chapter: PowerPyx's own table puts it in Chapter 4 in one place and lists Operation Savage Glory as a Chapter 5 mission in another; Insider Gaming lists the final mission under Chapter 5.
    • The Chapter 1 meditation: it raises the counter but is not marked as a Meditation Spot, and no source has confirmed whether Positive Mental Attitude needs it. All of them say to do it anyway.
    • Fair Game: which animal triggers the trophy is still unconfirmed; the 100% checklist needs all 15 Target Decks either way.
    • Bad Luck, I Guess: no confirmed method. This guide says so instead of guessing.

    How this guide is built

    The checklist and guides are plain files under app/. Trophy data lives in app/data/trophies.json: official name, description and grade, hidden and missable flags, kind, counts, and the guide with its sources. Progress is stored only in your browser. Nothing is fetched from the network.
